Feather PDF for Android
Created by Akshaya Simha. Free to use and share.

It contains PDFium (https://pdfium.googlesource.com/pdfium/), built by the pdfium-binaries project
(https://github.com/bblanchon/pdfium-binaries, release chromium/8066), with the libraries PDFium includes
(FreeType, libjpeg-turbo, libpng, zlib, OpenJPEG, Little CMS, Anti-Grain Geometry, ICU, Abseil, fast_float,
simdutf); and libc++ from the Android NDK. Their licence texts are listed beside this notice. All of them
are permissive licences.
