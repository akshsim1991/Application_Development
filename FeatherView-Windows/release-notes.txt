Feather View 2.1.0
- New app icon: a feather over a picture
- Opening a folder or drive shows every picture from all folders underneath in one go (scan runs in the background with a live count)
- Full screen covers the whole screen (Esc or F11 to leave); in videos the controls hide until you move the mouse; Space plays/pauses
- Option to keep a video playing when you switch tabs
- Smaller update downloads: a small installer (a couple of MB) for PCs that have the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime, and updates then stay small
- Tabs, details cards (camera, location, EXIF, histogram, text in picture), compare, slideshow shuffle
- Automatic update checks read this repository's releases
